How these are calculated

Recovery rate   = purified ÷ feed × 100
Reject fraction = reject ÷ feed × 100
Difference      = feed − purified − reject

A balance difference can come from measurement error, water retained in the unit, leaks or timing mismatch between sensors. Reject generated is what the purifier sends to drain; collected is what reached the tank; reused is what was logged for a non-drinking use.
